Option 1

Single cooler, limited SOW

$475per store

We deliver one KDP cooler to each store, starting Monday, November 16*. We expect to be first on site at most stores, so the scope covers both cases.

If Pepsi has already been on site

The old endcap is gone and one of the two new coolers is in place. We set the KDP cooler beside it and connect the two using the latch kit Pepsi left on site. The endcap is complete.

If we are first on site

We ask the manager on duty where they want the cooler: in its intended endcap position (empty), or in the back room. We leave it where they choose.

At every store

  • Record the cooler serial number.
  • Take photos of the cooler where it was left or installed.
  • Apply the stickers that ship with the cooler.
  • Submit the install data to KDP through the QR code.
  • Notify Pepsi that the store is ready, with a photo showing exactly where the cooler is, so any Pepsi crew coming in later can find it.

How the coolers move

  • All 68 KDP coolers ship to our staging point in Midlothian, Texas, and must be there by Friday, November 13*.
  • Both teams load out and drive to Missouri on Sunday, November 15*.
  • The teams carry every cooler they need for the whole route. There is no reload break in the middle of the project.

Not included in Option 1: the endcap conversion itself. That means removing the existing endcap or fixture, and removing, swapping or returning the old coolers, which stay on site for Pepsi. Also not included: setting the Celsius cooler, stocking, and the topper or header.

Option 1 schedule

All 68 stores in four days

Each team's route runs south to north on the road network between the actual store locations. We plan one hour on site at every store, plus drive time. The first stop of each day is at 9:00 am, and the work is spread evenly so each day wraps up around 7:30 to 8:00 pm, about two hours before the stores close at 10:00 pm. That cushion absorbs traffic, longer conversations with store managers, and anything unexpected on site. Teams stay overnight along their route, within an hour of the next morning's first store.

Option 2

Double cooler, full SOW

$595per store

We do the whole endcap conversion in one visit, following the full playbook. Pepsi's merchandising team comes in after us to set product.

At every store

  1. Remove the existing endcap.
  2. Take out the one or two existing Pepsi coolers and return them to Pepsi.
  3. Install both new coolers, KDP on the left and Celsius on the right, and connect them with the latch kit.
  4. Record serial numbers and photos, apply the stickers, and submit the install data to KDP through the QR code.
  5. Alert Pepsi that the endcap is installed so the merchandising team can come in and do their work.

Coolers and routing

  • Both coolers for every store (136 in total) can be delivered to one or two locations in Missouri.
  • We need to know where the coolers will be before we can build the actual daily route. That location sets where each day starts, when the teams reload, and where removed coolers go back.
  • Earliest start is Monday, November 16*, if coolers are staged by Friday, November 13*.

Not included in Option 2: stocking the coolers and installing the topper or header.

Dry runs

  • A dry run is a visit where we cannot complete the work for reasons outside our control, such as the store refusing the delivery or the site not being accessible.
  • Dry runs are billed at the same rate as a completed store.
  • We make re-delivery attempts as we are able. In Option 1, re-attempts happen on the north-to-south return sweep at the end of the project. In Option 2, they happen after the planned forward schedule.

Both options

  • Two teams work the same hours: first stop at 9:00 am, working through to store close.
  • Teams work weekends, since the stores are open Saturday and Sunday.
  • Every store gets serial numbers, photos, QR data to KDP, and a ready alert to Pepsi.

Schedule conditions

All dates in this proposal are proposed and estimated. They hold only if these conditions are met:

  • Coolers are delivered complete and on time: Option 1 coolers in Midlothian by Friday, November 13*; Option 2 coolers at the Missouri location(s) PSA names.
  • Each store is open and ready to receive the delivery when we arrive, and the store team expects us.
  • Where Pepsi has already started an endcap, the latch kit and first cooler are on site.
  • PSA confirms the option, the bottler contacts for ready alerts, and the QR code process before the start date.
  • No weather, road closures or other events outside our control delay the teams.

If a condition is not met, we will confirm revised dates with PSA as soon as we know.
